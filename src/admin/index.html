      Test on your own mailbox

      1. Open aka.ms/olksideload (Outlook on the web, My add-ins).
      2. Choose Custom Add-ins, then Add a custom add-in, Add from file.
      3. Select the downloaded manifest. It appears in classic Outlook, new Outlook and Mac within a few minutes.
      4. Write an email to an external address and click Send.

      Deploy to a pilot group

      1. Microsoft 365 admin center, Settings, Integrated apps, Upload custom apps.
      2. Choose Office Add-in and upload the manifest.
      3. Assign it to a pilot group. Remove SendGuard for the same users to avoid two prompts.
      4. Allow up to 24 hours for it to reach every mailbox.
